Privacy Policy

PostPilot · operated by Legendary Trading Academy, Marikina City, Philippines · Last updated: November 17, 2026

PostPilot is an internal tool that Legendary Trading Academy ("LTA", "we") uses to publish its own videos to its own social media accounts, including TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Telegram. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

  • TikTok: when an LTA administrator connects the academy's TikTok account, we receive the account's basic profile (open ID, display name and avatar) and an access token. We use them only to show which account is connected and to post the academy's own videos to that account.
  • Content we publish: videos and captions created by LTA, taken from LTA's own Google Drive and Google Sheet.
  • Messenger: messages people send to LTA's Facebook Page, used only to answer them and follow up on their inquiries.

How we use it

  • To publish LTA's videos at the scheduled times and show whether each post succeeded.
  • To show our team the performance of our own posts (views, likes, comments).
  • To respond to people who contact LTA.

We do not sell or rent any information, do not use it for advertising profiles, and do not access other TikTok users' data. PostPilot never edits, downloads or deletes content on TikTok or any other platform.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and settings are stored on LTA's own computer, in a private folder for the academy's workspace. They are not shared with third parties, except the platforms themselves (for example TikTok) when we publish to them.

How long we keep it

We keep connection tokens while the account is connected. An administrator can disconnect the account at any time; you can also remove PostPilot's access from your TikTok app settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

Your choices and requests

To ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to remove it, email admin@legendarytradingacademy.com. We reply within 7 days.
